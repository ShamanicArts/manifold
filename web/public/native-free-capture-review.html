inside the DAW.

The original two press capture action now crosses the real CLAP and VST3 editor bridges. The first press arms a Rust ring cursor at an audio block boundary. The second press measures the circular span, freezes the PCM in a prepared buffer, and publishes it to the Sample Instrument.
