Notes become wave and sample pitch

The old Main voice splits pitch between the waveform oscillator, raw sample speed, and the phase vocoder. A small Rust mapping reproduces those decisions across wave, sample, and both-keytrack modes. This checkpoint records the isolated reference trace; checkpoint 119 binds it to the playable graph.

Routing in the original voice

Desired sample ratioWhen sample keytrack is active, compare voice note frequency with root-note frequency. Multiply by the ±24 semitone pitch knob; clamp the result to 0.05–8.
Classic sample pitchSend the entire desired ratio to sample playback speed. Wave pitch follows the note, stays at the root, or also receives the pitch knob according to keytrack mode.
Vocoder pitchSend up to ±24 semitones into the vocoder. Any remaining coarse shift goes to sample playback speed. HQ selects the second vocoder mode.
