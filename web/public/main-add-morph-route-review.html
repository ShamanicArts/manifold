What these captures establish

Source spectral endpoint

Add and Morph use the old SineBankNode in its real spectral modes. Driven saw, bright, and two pulse-width recipes agree to float precision; the two pulse settings change assembled output by 0.1165 peak. Stretch and both tilt modes also change the Add source spectrum.

Morph curves and frequency depth

A slightly inharmonic published source makes frequency depth observable. Linear, cosine, and equal-power curves produce distinct output; raising depth from 0.25 to 1 moves settled output by 0.1801 peak. The original 20-band Add wavetable also matches saw, square, triangle and blend.
