An invalid sample or recipe is rejected while the live project remains available. A valid sample replacement produces audio and appears in saved state. Replacing a Main target changes its rendered sound and its saved target values. Replacing a Main source also keeps and rebuilds its temporal recipe.

Publishing a complete project resets active voices and DSP history. This is a project-load transition; the host still needs to schedule the swap at a block boundary and retire the previous instance outside its callback.
