Eight notes, eight places to play

The old Main keyboard allocates eight voice slots in its UI behavior. A different DSP module owns the eight voice graphs and receives indexed frequency, amplitude, and gate parameters. The Rust allocator now reproduces the keyboard's slot decisions; it is not yet connected to eight v2 Main audio graphs.

How a slot is chosen

1 · Idle slotUse the first inactive or idle slot. A repeated note can occupy another free slot.
2 · Quiet releaseWhen all eight slots are occupied, steal the releasing voice with the lowest envelope level.
3 · Oldest activeIf nothing is releasing, steal the oldest stamp. Note-off releases every active slot carrying that note.
