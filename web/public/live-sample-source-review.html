Change the sample
while it plays.

The Graph workbench's Sample Instrument now accepts a decoded audio file while its Rust/Wasm graph is running. The new PCM becomes the source for later notes. Notes already sounding finish on their previous source, and the replacement is saved in the portable project bundle.

30-second control cost

A synthetic Node worklet run replaced one 1,440,000-frame stereo source with another in 352 messages. The initial full-buffer reserve and final validation measured about 5.8 ms and 6.8 ms in earlier probes. With capacity reservation and per-chunk validation, a full-source replacement measured:

These are message-handler timings in a synthetic process. They exclude browser transfer scheduling, audio-device callbacks, and measured underruns.
