# Standalone EQ8 migration boundary

The original `UserScripts/projects/Standalone_Eq/manifold.project.json5` exports the eight-band `EQ8Node`, not the smaller three-band `EQNode` used elsewhere in the rack. The v2 [Standalone EQ8 project](../projects/standalone-eq8/project.json) follows that node and preserves the forty per-band controls plus output gain and dry/wet mix. All bands start disabled; band 1 defaults to a low shelf at 60 Hz with Q 0.8, bands 2–7 to bell filters, and band 8 to a high shelf. The band tabs keep five controls at a time visible without shrinking the full parameter set.

The Rust DSP implements the original seven biquad modes, 10 ms parameter smoothing, coefficient update thresholds, per-channel filter state, serial band order, and dry bypass policy. Coefficients and delay states live in a prepared graph kernel; no allocation occurs in the audio callback. Graph authored values are installed before processing so the first frame is comparable to the C++ prepare state. A block-boundary parameter update in the workbench can smoothly change frequency, gain, Q, output, and mix, or switch band enable/type.

Nine deterministic stereo fixtures come directly from the old `EQ8Node.cpp` through `tools/legacy-eq8-reference.cpp`. The cases cover disabled passthrough, low and high shelves, bell, notch, band pass, low and high pass, enabling a band, all eight bands, short blocks, and output/mix changes. They are compared with Rust/Wasm in the browser. This is C++ node parity evidence; full preset/OSC roundtrip and the original Lua UI are not imported. The rack's separate `EQNode` and `FilterNode` are still outstanding.
