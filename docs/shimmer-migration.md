# Shimmer migration boundary

The original `dsp/core/nodes/ShimmerNode.cpp` uses a three-second stereo delay ring, a slow LFO that offsets the two read positions in opposite directions, and a read step of `2^(pitch/12)`. Its filtered feedback is written into the delay ring; the pitched sample is sent directly to the wet output. Rust graph kind 50 ports that processing order, per-sample 10 ms smoothing, and all six parameters. The [workbench](../projects/shimmer/project.json) exposes size, pitch, feedback, mix, modulation, and filter cutoff. Prepared storage and generation stamps let an FX type switch clear delay history without allocating on the audio callback.

Eight checked-in C++ captures cover the default, short delay, pitch and size sweeps, feedback/filter, stereo modulation, mix, and 64-frame processing. All eight match Rust/Wasm with zero maximum sample difference in these cases. Standalone FX type 18 maps normalized `p/0` to size `0.1…1`, `p/1` to pitch `−12…12`, `p/2` to feedback `0…0.99`, and `p/3` exponentially to `100…12000 Hz`; `p/4` is unused. As in the Lua definition, internal mix stays at `0.5`, modulation at `0.25`, and the slot adds 1.4× wet gain. Four new slot cases compare native Rust with Rust/Wasm. Project-level routing and preset roundtrips remain separate work.
