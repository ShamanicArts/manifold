## What the images actually do

These are still frames. They establish composition and suggested forces, not
evidence of a particular animation or simulation.

1. A tightly cropped, frontal face combines an ordinary lower face with an
   oversized upper eye. White wire topology describes anatomy across reflective
   polygonal skin. Crystals and gold frame the head; straight coloured beams
   cross the image. Small technical windows and text sit around this intensely
   bodily centre. Recognisable anatomy makes the excess legible.
2. A pale head with an open mouth floats against densely branching gold,
   jewel-like forms and repeated faces. Its black graphic outline and the flat
   rainbow ribbon differ sharply from the reflective rendered structures.
   The deliberate collision of illustration and 3D gives it play and unease.
3. A humanoid with extended arms anchors the composition. Its fragmented body
   and circular head structure sit amid repeated forms and radiating lines.
   The apparent folded surround adds another plane of presentation. Body,
   architecture and image frame coexist at different scales.
4. A comparatively spare humanoid has a bright chest square. Branching lines
   connect the torso to the surrounding space, while columns of characters
   form a larger field around it. Light, anatomy and information share a
   structure. The chest is a clear origin for the depicted activity.

## Feeling, texture and space

My interpretation is ecstatic, synthetic and bodily, with sacred symmetry,
ornament, humour and some discomfort. Technical imagery is intimate and
expressive. There is warmth in the gold and flesh-like forms, and aggression in
the needles, fractures and extreme facial expression.

Lucas confirmed the church-artifact/Christ-figure and futuristic-technology
tension as useful grounding. Translate the craftsmanship, symmetry and material
relationships into the device. Keep both tones of each Manifold slider
expressive; a single accent across every control misses the kit's possibilities.

The material vocabulary has strong differences: smooth wet-looking skin,
angular mineral facets, thin gold branches, fine wire grids, flat graphic
colour and dense small text. Much of the colour appears in reflections,
refraction-like facets and narrow luminous paths. The rainbow ribbon also uses
solid colour deliberately. A uniformly tinted surface would lose these roles.

Space is dense and layered. Forms overlap, repeat, extend beyond the crop and
switch between fine detail and large identifiable shapes. Dark gaps let bright
edges separate. The subject occupies the frame decisively. A distant small
object on an empty floor loses that spatial character.

## Translation into this device

The plugin pane is the device's face. Its physical construction should own the
viewport: shaped edges, seams, inset controls, chamber, ports and cable exits.
Use cropping and overlap to imply what continues outside it. Avoid spending
most of the pane on a presentation floor or distant environment.

The device can express bodily qualities through swollen polished contours,
branching conductive paths, membrane-like liquid and mineral growth. Literal
heads, eyes or crowns are not required. Every addition must strengthen the
device as a whole.

Connections are part of the composition. Explore chrome/gold port fittings and
rainbow optical braids that visibly enter and leave the panel. Their activity
should follow actual source/output state and analysis. Distinguish connection
indication from any implemented routing interaction.

The control area deserves authored shape and materials. Keep Manifold's filled
slider interaction, then integrate its channels and buttons into the surface.
Give functional groups distinct colour roles and make colour recur in their
associated material paths. A generic green rectangle beside an elaborate
chamber does not satisfy this brief.

The chamber should have visible depth and continuous relationships between
liquid, nodal grains, filament structure and facets. Sound should produce
specific readable changes. Touch should disturb the point of contact promptly
while still changing the real graph parameters during the gesture.

## Instancing and craft

Instancing is a rendering method, not a visual arrangement. Preserve or improve
silhouette, placement, scale, orientation, depth, colour, surface response and
hierarchy. Crystal populations need authored growth directions, clustered
variation and room between groups. Uniform beads around a circle can read as
cheap ornament regardless of draw-call count.

Compare equivalent views before and after optimization. Inspect close detail,
the whole composition, sounding movement and held touch on the real build.
Reject a faster result if it visibly weakens the intended material or form.

## Next reviewable step

Build and inspect the device composition first: pane framing, surface,
connections and control integration, with the existing sound/field behavior.
Establish a convincing whole before elaborating chamber geometry. Share an
actual live build and images; identify unfinished portions directly. Avoid
claiming that keyword coverage, a performance number or decorative density
demonstrates artistic success.

## Canonical wide interaction, 10 October 2026

Lucas chose the approximately 1300 × 750 plugin face as the reference wide
composition. The portal occupies the left; the hand and its crystal sit above
the control deck on the right. Responsive layouts may reflow, but changes to
the wide face should be judged at this size before shipping.

Every surface used as a control must move with the accepted parameter, whether
it was changed by touch, a Manifold slider, a preset, or an agent. Portal drag
adjusts Focus and Displacement, which move its iris and engraved index. The
crystal in the hand adjusts Grain and Scatter, moving its inner bearings and
the wrist anchored hand. The outer collar adjusts Transmutation and Afterimage;
its turn and expansion follow those values, and Afterimage changes the hand's
grasp pose. The eye can follow the cursor; its hold state remains a graph
parameter. Short pose transitions should read as deliberate image states,
without endless decorative wobble. Graphics must never own a second DSP value.

## Shell creature on the field rim

**User correction, 11 October:** Lucas requested the shell creature resting or
walking on the circle. He had explicitly liked the existing synaptic/reliquary
structure and asked for animation; removing it was not authorized. The replacement
described below is implementation history, not an approved composition decision.
His silence afterward was not acceptance. The original structure and its assets
remain recoverable at revision `e1dc85a39229`; do not make further composition
changes as part of a technical repair.

The implementation removed the hand at the end of the old bridge art. One mechanical
crab with a pearlescent nautilus shell and skull face now grips the portal rim.
Three registered cutouts show rest, a step with lit eyes, and an open mouth;
frames switch discretely so two silhouettes never show through one another.
The skull and mouth are a drag target for Prism Edge (horizontal) and Prism Heat
(vertical), using the same parameter paths as the panel and inspector. Its
mouth light and pose respond to those accepted values and output analysis.
Without the Prism module, the same target operates Focus and Excitation.
The separate crystal hand remains draggable across its visible alpha silhouette
for Grain and Scatter, with its halo handling Mix and Afterimage.
